API Reference¶

Images are PyTorch tensors with values in [0, 1], of shape (H, W) or (B, H, W), on CPU or GPU.

Pretrained Priors¶

Priors shipped with the package: MAP priors on 485 analytic filters (31 x 31) with monotone potentials, fitted in closed form on the 400 BSD training images (8 Gauss-Newton steps). Use them with denoise() at their noise level, or as regularisers for inverse problems.

Fitting¶

explicit_priors.fit(filters, images, sigma=None, objective='mse', basis=None, groups=None, shape=None, val=0.1, reg='auto', refine=0, batch=None, device=None, seed=0, verbose=False)[source]¶

Fit the potentials of a fields of experts in closed form.

Parameters:

  • filters (Tensor | FoE) – (K, s, s) filters (see filters), or a prior whose filters, basis and groups are reused.

  • images (Tensor | list) – Clean training images in [0, 1]: (N, H, W), or a list of (H, W).

  • sigma (float) – Noise std of the denoiser (“mse”, “map”).

  • objective (str) – “score” (prior of clean images), “mse” (one-step denoiser) or “map” (MAP denoiser).

  • basis (Hat | Gauss) – Potential basis (default: Hat).

  • groups (Tensor) – (K,) potential index of every filter, e.g. from symmetrize().

  • shape (str) – “free”, “monotone” (even, non-decreasing in \(|z|\)) or “convex”. Default: “monotone” for “map”, “free” otherwise.

  • val (float) – Fraction of the images held out to choose the regularisation.

  • reg (str | tuple) – “auto”, or (ridge, smooth).

  • refine (int) – Gauss–Newton steps on the MAP denoiser (“map” only).

  • batch (int) – Images per batch (default: from the problem size).

  • device (str) – Default: CUDA if available.

Returns:

prior

Return type:

Learn filters for the one-step denoiser at noise sigma, with the potentials solved at every step.

Parameters:

  • images (Tensor | list) – Clean training images in [0, 1], at least crop x crop.

  • n_filters (int) – Number of filters K.

  • size (int) – Filter support s.

  • sigma (float) – Noise std.

  • steps (int) – Gradient steps (Adam, cosine learning-rate decay).

  • batch (int) – Random crops per step and their size.

  • crop (int) – Random crops per step and their size.

  • lr (float) – Learning rate.

  • whiten (bool) – Parametrise the filters in whitened patch coordinates (better conditioned).

  • init (Tensor) – (K, s, s) initial filters (default: random).

  • restarts (int) – Times the least useful filters (value < 0.1 x max) are re-initialised.

Returns:

filters – (K, s, s) zero-mean, unit-norm filters, ready for fit().

Denoising and Inverse Problems¶

explicit_priors.denoise(prior, y, sigma=None, **kwargs)[source]¶

one_step() for objective="mse" priors, map_estimate() otherwise.

explicit_priors.one_step(prior, y, sigma=None, pad=None)[source]¶

One-step (Tweedie) denoiser \(y - \sigma^2 \nabla \Phi(y)\).

Parameters:

  • prior (FoE) – Prior, usually fitted with objective="mse".

  • y (Tensor) – (H, W) or (B, H, W) noisy images.

  • sigma (float) – Noise std (default: prior.sigma).

  • pad (int) – Reflect padding (default: filter size - 1).

explicit_priors.map_estimate(prior, y, sigma=None, beta=None, iters=100, x0=None, pad=16)[source]¶

MAP denoiser \(\arg\min_x \tfrac12 \|x - y\|^2 + \beta \Phi(x)\).

Parameters:

  • prior (FoE) – Prior, usually fitted with objective="map".

  • y (Tensor) – (H, W) or (B, H, W) noisy images.

  • sigma (float) – Noise std (default: prior.sigma).

  • beta (float) – Weight of the prior (default: \(\sigma^2\), the Bayesian weight; no tuning needed for objective="map").

  • iters (int) – FISTA iterations.

  • x0 (Tensor) – Initial point (default: y).

  • pad (int) – Reflect padding.

Potential Bases¶

class explicit_priors.Hat(n=28, eps=0.002)[source]¶

Piecewise-linear \(\psi\): hat functions on knots \(0 = t_0 < t_1 < \dots < t_n = 1\), geometric in \((\epsilon, 1]\), continued beyond \(|u| = 1\) by a linear tail.

Coefficients \([v_1, \dots, v_n, w]\) are the values of \(\psi\) at the knots and the slope of the tail.

Parameters:

  • n (int) – Number of knots.

  • eps (float) – First knot, relative to the scale.

class explicit_priors.Gauss(n=24, eps=0.0015)[source]¶

Smooth \(\psi\): odd Gaussian bumps \(e^{-(u - \mu_a)^2 / 2 h_a^2} - e^{-(u + \mu_a)^2 / 2 h_a^2}\) on geometric centres \(\mu_a \in [\epsilon, 1]\), plus a linear term \(u\).

Parameters:

  • n (int) – Number of bumps.

  • eps (float) – First centre, relative to the scale.

explicit_priors.normal_equations(prior, pairs)[source]¶

A, d of the least-squares problem, averaged over pixels.
